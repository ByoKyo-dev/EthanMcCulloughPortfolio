About

I’m a designer who cares as much about how a piece prints as how it looks on screen.

For the past two and a half years I’ve developed visual concepts, worked directly with clients, and prepared production-ready artwork. My strengths are typography, color management, visual consistency and print production.

At Don’t Drive Naked I present custom concepts and guide clients through feedback, manage color workflows and client color matching, and prepare final files for print through RIP software. I also run the production equipment, so I see each job through printing and lamination to final quality control.
